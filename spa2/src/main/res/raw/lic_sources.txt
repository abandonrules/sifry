Calvin and Hobbes (Lookup packs)

  "Calvin and Hobbes" was created by Bill Watterson and ran as a daily strip
  from 18 November 1985 to 31 December 1995. It is copyright (c) Bill
  Watterson, published by Andrews McMeel / Universal Uclick.

  The Lookup packs data set bundled with SPA2 is a reference index of the
  strip's cast, recurring devices and themes, and short quotations, each with
  the date of the strip it comes from. Quotations are transcribed from the
  WIST Quotations index (https://wist.info/author/watterson-bill/), which
  cites the GoComics archive of the strip for every entry; strip dates follow
  the GoComics archive dates given by that index. Quotations are short
  excerpts used for identification and reference, and remain the property of
  their rights holder. No artwork, artwork scans or longer passages are
  included, and nothing here is endorsed by or affiliated with the rights
  holder.
